Overview

My role as a conversation designer spanned over the past 8 months on a number of projects for the Xfinity Assistant. Xfinity Assistant is a customer facing chatbot that helps fix troubleshoot internet, tv issues, help pay and answer questions about bills, make technician and store appointments, etc.

I helped with the following tasks: create designs for the assistant that reflected potential user experience and actual vision strategy and created designs that were released into production. The second task I helped with was creating content guidelines for the assistant. Finally, I helped review transcripts and provided design recommendations for the Xfinity assistant.

Creating Conversation Designs

First, I created designs for a future state of the billing section to showcase how we could grow the agent out. I was able to work with a UX strategist to create a user flow of a happy path for the agent from beginning to end. I was also able to help create the content for legal disclaimer for the agent as well to make it more marketable and easier for teams to understand. I also worked on two designs for the escalation agent.

The first design was to update the messaging and prioritize the fastest options for users. There were three transfer options, getting someone to a live agent, having a live agent call a user back as soon as they can and the user can schedule a callback. The second design I worked on was redesigning the callbacks so that customer could select their phone number, preferred day and time and also be notified what number the live agent will call on. I also provided designs for cancelling and rescheduling the callbacks. I also created a vision strategy to showcase what the assistant would look like if it could as a general LLM. This design was taken to legal for review.

Creating LLM/Content Guardrails

Next, I helped with creating guidelines/guardrails. I worked with my team to create the content guidelines for each section of the agent. I also worked on finding best case examples to highlight content guidelines for reference. Additionally I worked with another content designer to facilitate group discussions on inconsistencies found in the chatbot. I also helped organized guidelines/guardrails and help provide additional examples and rules for our IVR guidelines. Finally, I created guidelines dedicated to escalating users to live agents.

Screenshot of Prompt with Chatbot

Analyzing Transcripts

Finally, I helped worked on the optimization team to review transcripts and present findings to stakeholders. During my time, I helped evaluate LLMs for retrieving transcripts and I also helped find different issues related to the billing and collections sections. I also helped with creating guidelines for rating issues based on severity metrics from the business.

Conclusion

Overall, this experience taught me how to work with a new platform and learn how to work on the technical side of conversational AI. I was also able to learn about the financial industry and experience working with multiple types of platforms.
